Live transcript

Start the mic (top right) to begin. Everything the Coach panel knows — the problem, the approach, any code talked through — comes from this transcript, so the more that gets said out loud, the better its answers are. Works best in Chrome. Entirely local to this tab — nothing is recorded outside your session.

"Capture call audio" adds the other person's voice from a Meet/Teams/Zoom browser tab into this same transcript — pick that tab and check "Share tab audio" when Chrome prompts. It only reads a copy of that tab's audio; your speakers keep playing it normally. Needs a Whisper-compatible transcription server configured (see README) — without one, this button will just error.

Coach

"Solve" writes out a full example answer in the candidate's own voice — the kind of thing a strong candidate would actually say out loud, code and all. Switch tabs for a smaller hint, a correctness check, or a complexity read instead. Hit "Ask coach", or turn on auto-answer below to have it fire on its own after a natural pause.
Auto-answer on pause (P)
On — after the candidate goes quiet for a few seconds, Solve runs automatically and its answer appears above. Visible to everyone watching this screen, same as a manual click.

End-of-session debrief
